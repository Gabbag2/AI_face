Forehead sensor placement

This app shows where to put your EEG sensor on the forehead (Fp1–Fp2, AF7–AF8, F7–F8…), draws it on your face and tells you how to move it.

  1. Pull your hair away from the forehead, remove glasses.
  2. Hold the phone at arm's length, face the light.
  3. Follow the arrows until the outline turns green.

The video never leaves your device.
